## 5. Social & Ethical Dimensions

### Data Privacy & Security  
- **PDPB (Personal Data Protection Bill)**: 2023 draft introduces data localization, consent, and data fiduciary roles.  
- **Breaches**: 2022 Aadhaar data leak affected 3 million users; led to stricter biometric safeguards.

### Bias & Fairness  
- **Hiring**: An AI‑powered recruitment platform in 2021 flagged a 15% gender bias in candidate scoring.  
- **Credit Scoring**: A fintech startup’s model was found to under‑score rural applicants, prompting regulatory scrutiny.

### Job Displacement Concerns  
- **High‑risk sectors**: Call centers, retail cashiers, and basic manufacturing.  
- **Mitigation**: Upskilling programs, job‑rotation strategies, and universal basic income pilots.

### Digital Literacy & Inclusion  
- **Gender Gap**: Only 21% of women in India have internet access (2023).  
- **Regional Gap**: Rural AI literacy rate is 18% lower than urban.  
- **Initiatives**: “AI for All” NGO program, government “Digital India” drives.
